AI Opportunity & Ownership Lab

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing how we work and learn. But many young people use AI without knowing who owns the results or how their personal data is being used by big tech companies.

What you will learn here:

  • Who owns your data: Understand how to protect your personal information when using AI tools.
  • AI and Jobs: Learn how to work *with* AI to get better jobs, rather than being replaced by it.
  • Ownership: If you create something using AI, who owns the copyright? We will explain this in simple terms.

Status: Content and learning guides are currently in development.

Creator Rights & Protection Lab

If you are a musician, artist, writer, or content creator, the moment you make something, it belongs to you. But the internet can be risky. People steal content, and platforms sometimes ban accounts unfairly.

What you will learn here:

  • Copyright Basics: What the law says about your original work and how to prove you made it first.
  • Platform Risks: How to avoid getting your account banned and what to do if it happens.
  • Reporting Theft: Step-by-step guides on how to take down stolen content and report abuse to platforms.

Status: Checklists and reporting templates are being planned.

Internet Affordability & Access Lab

Data is expensive, and network connections can be slow. This makes it very hard for many young people to learn online, find jobs, or start businesses. We believe the internet should be accessible to everyone.

What you will learn here:

  • Save Your Data: Practical tips to stop apps from wasting your data in the background.
  • Find Better Access: How to find and use public Wi-Fi safely without getting hacked.
  • Your Rights: Understanding what internet access you are entitled to and how to speak up for better networks.

Status: Data-saving guides are planned for Stage 2.

Youth Internet Governance Lab

"Internet Governance" sounds like a difficult word, but it just means "the rules of the internet." Right now, older people and big companies are making the rules, and the voices of young Nigerians are often ignored.

What you will learn here:

  • How Rules Are Made: A simple explanation of how global internet policies are created.
  • Why Your Voice Matters: How decisions made far away affect your daily life and data costs.
  • How to Participate: Steps you can take to join youth networks and have a real say in your digital future.

Status: Governance explainers are in the design phase.

Cybersecurity & Online Safety Centre

Hackers, scammers, and fake links are everywhere. If your account gets hacked, you can lose your money, your photos, and even your reputation. But you don't need to be a tech expert to stay safe.

What you will learn here:

  • Strong Passwords: How to create passwords that hackers cannot guess (and why you should never reuse them).
  • Spotting Scams: How to recognize fake emails, phishing links, and "too good to be true" offers.
  • Account Security: How to turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to lock your accounts completely.

Status: Safety checklists and scam-awareness guides are planned.

Digital Skills Academy

Many young people want to learn tech skills like coding, graphic design, or digital marketing, but they don't know where to start. The internet is full of confusing information, and some courses are too expensive.

What you will learn here:

  • Where to Start: Clear, beginner-friendly learning paths so you know exactly what to learn first.
  • Free Resources: A collected list of the best free websites, videos, and tools to learn digital skills.
  • Practical Skills: How to use everyday tools like Google Workspace, Canva, and basic coding to solve real problems.

Status: The learning pathway structure is being planned.

Youth Internet Observatory

We cannot fix the internet if we don't know what is actually happening to young people online. Are you being bullied? Is your data too expensive? Are you seeing harmful content? We need to hear your real experiences.

What you will learn here:

  • Understanding the Reality: How to safely observe and document the challenges youth face online.
  • Anonymity: How to share your experiences without revealing your personal identity or getting into trouble.
  • Finding Solutions: How this data will be used to create better safety rules and policies for Nigeria.

Status: Concept only. No personal data is collected right now.

Innovation & Collaboration Hub

Building a safer internet cannot be done by one person alone. It requires teamwork between young developers, artists, writers, and organizations who all want to make the internet a better place.

What you will learn here:

  • Team Up: How to find other young people who share your passion for digital trust and innovation.
  • Share Ideas: A safe space to propose new project ideas that can solve community problems.
  • Build Together: How to collaborate on real projects, share skills, and grow your network for future opportunities.

Status: Collaboration frameworks are being designed.
